Welcome to Eleora Engineers

Eleora Electricals & Engineers Private Limited is a Class I Electrical Contracting & Engineering resource firm based in Bangalore that has a proven track of success in taking numerous high profile projects from concept to commissioning. With years of experience and a strong set of values, we consistently provide the technical and professional expertise to execute the most complex projects in Karnataka, Chennai & Hydreabad.

We had served a wide variety of customers like IT, Labs, Data Centres, R & D Centres, Pharma Labs, Commercial Offices, Industries, Ware Houses , Garment Factories, Educational Institutions, Banks,Hotels & Cafeteria . Since year 2006, we have quietly grown from a small suburb electrical contractor to a large Electrical firm.

We are the Class 1 Contractors & Engineers registered in Karnataka with compliance to meet all the Statutory requirements. For Agile and Ontime Delivery of Goods to our Site we have our own Transport. We are complied with highly skilled and dedicated onsite workforce which includes Project Managers, Project Engineers, Site Supervisors , Electricans & Helper.The Entire process flow is well designed and monitored thoroughly for an effective & timely results.

we have a long record of successful projects, we are proud of our well-earned reputation of high quality work. Our commitment to excellence is not just a way of doing business but a way of life.We provide Timely Quality Services and solutions in the field of electrical installations and maintenance.
